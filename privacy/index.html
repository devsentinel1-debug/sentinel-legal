1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Sentinel (“Sentinel,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the “App”). Sentinel is a hunting and hiking safety application designed for use in New Zealand, providing features including location tracking, zone and trail mapping, safety check-ins, SOS alerts, hunting and hiking data, and AI-powered tools.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly. This Policy is written to comply with New Zealand’s Privacy Act 2020, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), Apple’s App Store guidelines, and Google Play’s Data Safety requirements. If you are located outside New Zealand, additional rights described in Section 11 may apply to you.

By downloading, accessing, or using Sentinel, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account information: name, email address, password (encrypted), and profile details you choose to add.
  • Content you create: hunt logs, sightings, photos, notes, game log entries, zone boundaries, waypoints, trip plans, and messages sent to AI features (such as Sentinel Companion).
  • Communications: feedback, support requests, and survey responses you send us.
  • Payment information: when you purchase a subscription or in-app item, payment is processed by Apple App Store, Google Play, or RevenueCat. We do not directly collect or store your full payment card details; we receive confirmation of purchase and entitlement status only.

2.2 Location Information

Location data is central to Sentinel’s safety features, and we want to be especially clear about how it works:

  • Precise location (GPS): We collect your precise real-time location when you use mapping, tracking, zone detection, hiker check-in, SOS, and related features. This may be collected while the App is in use, and — if you grant “Always Allow” / background location permission — while the App is running in the background, in order to support continuous tracking, SOS alerts, and proximity warnings (such as hiker-route-to-hunt-zone warnings) even when the App is not actively open.
  • Recorded tracks and waypoints: routes, hunt zones, walking tracks, and waypoints you record or draw are stored against your account.
  • Approximate location: derived from IP address or network information for general purposes such as showing relevant regional content.

You can control location permissions at any time through your device’s operating system settings (iOS Settings > Privacy > Location Services; Android Settings > Apps > Permissions > Location). Disabling location access will prevent core safety features — including SOS, zone detection, hiker check-in, and live tracking — from functioning correctly. We do not recommend disabling location access while you are out in the field, as this may compromise your safety.

Sharing location with other people. Certain features (such as hiker check-in and SOS) are designed to share your location with emergency contacts you nominate, or with Sentinel’s systems for the purpose of alerting others if you do not check in as expected. We do not make your live location publicly visible to other app users by default.

Community and research location data. As described in Section 4.3, de-identified or aggregated location data (such as sighting locations and hunt zone activity) may be contributed to community heatmaps and shared with research and government partners. This is a mandatory part of using certain community features, and is separate from your precise, identifiable real-time location described above.

2.3 Photos, Camera, and Media

If you use Species ID, Field Journal, or photo attachment features, we access your camera and/or photo library to allow you to capture or upload images (for example, of game animals, sightings, or gear). Photos you submit for AI species identification may be processed by our AI service provider as described in Section 5.

2.4 Device and Usage Information

  • Device model, operating system and version, unique device identifiers, IP address, app version, crash logs, and diagnostic data.
  • Usage data such as features used, screens viewed, session duration, and in-app actions (e.g., badges earned, credits spent).

2.5 Health and Biometric Information

If you enable biometric login (fingerprint or face recognition), authentication is handled entirely by your device’s operating system (Apple Face ID/Touch ID, Android biometric APIs). Sentinel does not receive, store, or have access to your biometric data itself — only a confirmation that authentication succeeded or failed.

2.6 Information from Third Parties

  • DOC (Department of Conservation) and Fish & Game NZ data: publicly available track, zone, and hunting information we incorporate into the App is not personal information about you, but your interactions with it (e.g., favourites, visited tracks) are linked to your account.
  • LINZ (Land Information New Zealand): property boundary and topographic data used for map overlays; this is not personal information about you.
  • Weather and environmental data providers (such as NIWA and OpenWeatherMap): used to provide forecasts and conditions; not linked to your identity.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide core App functionality: mapping, zone detection, tracking, SOS alerts, hiker check-ins, and safety notifications.
  • Operate AI-powered features (Sentinel Companion, Hunt Report, Safety Assessment, Hunt Debrief, Gear Recommender, Trip Planner, and Species ID), as described in Section 5.
  • Maintain your account, process subscriptions and purchases, and manage the in-app credit system.
  • Award badges, track milestones, and manage gamified features.
  • Improve, troubleshoot, and secure the App, including crash reporting and analytics.
  • Communicate with you about your account, safety-related notices, and (where you have consented) product updates.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from authorities.
  • With your consent (Section 4.3), contribute aggregated or de-identified data to wildlife and conservation research.

We do not use your personal information to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you without human involvement.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

4.1 Service Providers

We use third-party service providers to operate the App, including:

  • Supabase (database hosting and backend infrastructure)
  • Anthropic (AI model processing for AI-powered features — see Section 5)
  • RevenueCat (subscription and in-app purchase management)
  • Apple App Store / Google Play (app distribution and payment processing)
  • Sentry (crash reporting and error diagnostics)
  • Expo / push notification services (delivering notifications)

These providers process data on our behalf and are contractually restricted from using your information for their own purposes.

4.2 Emergency Contacts and Safety Features

If you use SOS or hiker check-in features, we share relevant location and status information with the emergency contacts you designate, and may share information with emergency services if reasonably necessary to protect your life or safety or that of others.

4.3 Research and Government Partners — Mandatory Community Contribution

Sentinel includes a single “Contribute to Research” consent setting, presented once during onboarding. If enabled, aggregated or de-identified sighting and activity data (such as species sightings, general hunt zone activity, and walking track usage) may be shared with the Department of Conservation (DOC), Fish & Game New Zealand, and similar research bodies, to support wildlife management and conservation efforts. This consent covers all such data sharing as a single setting; we do not currently offer feature-by-feature opt-outs, because partial data significantly reduces the scientific and safety value of community data for all users. Where reasonably practicable, data shared for research purposes is aggregated or de-identified before disclosure. You may decline this consent during onboarding, which will not prevent you from using core safety, mapping, or tracking features, but community-contribution features built specifically for data sharing may not be available to you.

4.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Sentinel, our users, or the public.

4.5 Business Transfers

If Sentinel is involved in a merger, acquisition, asset sale, or similar transaction, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change.

5. AI Features and Your Data

Sentinel provides several AI-assisted features (Sentinel Companion, Hunt Report, Safety Assessment, Hunt Debrief, Gear Recommender, Trip Planner, and Species ID). To provide these features:

  • Relevant information you provide (such as chat messages, hunt details, location and weather context, or photos for species identification) is sent to our AI infrastructure provider, Anthropic, for processing.
  • We do not use your individual, identifiable conversations or submissions to train Anthropic’s underlying AI models, and our agreements with Anthropic restrict the use of API data for that purpose.
  • AI-generated outputs (such as safety assessments or gear recommendations) may be cached for efficiency and to avoid redundant processing, linked to the relevant zone, track, or query rather than to you personally where feasible.
  • AI features that involve photo submission (Species ID) only process images you actively choose to submit for that purpose.
  • AI outputs are generated by a machine-learning model and may occasionally be inaccurate or incomplete. AI-generated safety assessments, hunt debriefs, gear recommendations, and trip plans are provided for informational purposes only and should not be relied upon as a substitute for your own judgment, official safety advice, or emergency services.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App’s services. Specifically:

  • Account and profile data: retained until you delete your account.
  • Location tracks, zones, and waypoints: retained until you delete them or delete your account, unless retention is required for safety, legal, or dispute-resolution purposes.
  • AI conversation history and cached AI outputs: retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant feature, and may be periodically purged.
  • Crash logs and diagnostic data: typically retained for up to 90 days.
  • Aggregated or de-identified research data: may be retained indefinitely, as it is no longer reasonably linkable to you.

When you delete your account, we will delete or de-identify your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it by law or for legitimate business purposes such as fraud prevention or dispute resolution.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encryption in transit, database-level access controls, and row-level security on our backend (Supabase/PostgreSQL). Biometric authentication, where enabled, never leaves your device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If we become aware of a privacy or security breach that has caused, or is likely to cause, serious harm, we will notify affected individuals and the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner in accordance with the Privacy Act 2020, and will notify other regulators or individuals as required by applicable law (including GDPR’s 72-hour breach notification requirement where relevant).

8. International Data Transfers

Sentinel is based in New Zealand, but some of our service providers (including Supabase and Anthropic) may process or store data outside New Zealand, including in the United States or other jurisdictions. Where we transfer personal information internationally, we take reasonable steps to ensure it receives a comparable standard of protection, including through contractual safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where applicable to users in the EU/UK.

9. Children’s Privacy

Sentinel is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. Hunting and certain hiking activities may be subject to age restrictions under New Zealand law independent of this Policy. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without appropriate consent, we will take steps to delete that information. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details in Section 13.

10. Your Privacy Rights (New Zealand)

Under the Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct personal information you believe is inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date.
  • Be informed about what personal information is collected and why, including when collected indirectly (in accordance with Information Privacy Principle 3A).

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13. We will respond as soon as reasonably practicable, and in any case within 20 working days, as required by law. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

11. Additional Rights for Users Outside New Zealand

11.1 European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland (GDPR/UK GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, to data portability, to object to certain processing (including for direct marketing), and to withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent. Our legal bases for processing include performance of a contract (providing the App), consent (e.g., research data contribution, optional notifications), and legitimate interests (e.g., security, fraud prevention, service improvement). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11.2 California (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose; to request deletion of your personal information; to correct inaccurate personal information; and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. To the extent our sharing of aggregated research data with third parties is considered “sharing” under the CCPA, you may opt out via the “Contribute to Research” setting described in Section 4.3, or by contacting us. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

11.3 Other Jurisdictions

If you reside in a jurisdiction with privacy laws not specifically addressed above (such as Australia’s Privacy Act 1988 or Canada’s PIPEDA), we aim to honour requests consistent with the rights described in this Policy to the extent reasonably practicable. Contact us using the details in Section 13.

12. Apple and Google Platform Disclosures

12.1 Apple App Store

In accordance with Apple’s App Privacy requirements, our App Privacy “nutrition label” in the App Store discloses the categories of data collected (including location, contact info, identifiers, usage data, diagnostics, and user content) and whether that data is linked to your identity or used for tracking. Sentinel does not use data for cross-app/cross-website tracking as defined by Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework, and accordingly does not require ATT consent for that purpose. Permissions requested (such as location “Always” and “While Using,” camera, photo library, and notifications) are used solely for the purposes described in this Policy.

12.2 Google Play

In accordance with Google Play’s Data Safety requirements, our Play Store listing discloses the types of data collected and shared, our security practices (including encryption in transit and the ability to request data deletion), and whether data collection is optional. Background location access is requested only to support core safety features described in Section 2.2, consistent with Google Play’s location permissions policy.
