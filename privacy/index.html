Introduction This Privacy Policy explains how Sentinel ("Sentinel," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "App"). Sentinel is a hunting and hiking safety application designed for use in New Zealand, providing features including location tracking, zone and trail mapping, safety check-ins, SOS alerts, hunting and hiking data, and AI-powered tools. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly. This Policy is written to comply with New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), Apple's App Store guidelines, and Google Play's Data Safety requirements. If you are located outside New Zealand, additional rights described in Section 11 may apply to you. By downloading, accessing, or using Sentinel, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the App. ## 2. Information We Collect We collect the following categories of information: ### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly - **Account information**: name, email address, password (encrypted), and profile details you choose to add. - **Content you create**: hunt logs, sightings, photos, notes, game log entries, zone boundaries, waypoints, trip plans, and messages sent to AI features (such as Sentinel Companion). - **Communications**: feedback, support requests, and survey responses you send us. - **Payment information**: when you purchase a subscription or in-app item, payment is processed by Apple App Store, Google Play, or RevenueCat. We do not directly collect or store your full payment card details; we receive confirmation of purchase and entitlement status only. ### 2.2 Location Information Location data is central to Sentinel's safety features, and we want to be especially clear about how it works: - **Precise location (GPS)**: We collect your precise real-time location when you use mapping, tracking, zone detection, hiker check-in, SOS, and related features. This may be collected while the App is in use, and — if you grant "Always Allow" / background location permission — while the App is running in the background, in order to support continuous tracking, SOS alerts, and proximity warnings (such as hiker-route-to-hunt-zone warnings) even when the App is not actively open. - **Recorded tracks and waypoints**: routes, hunt zones, walking tracks, and waypoints you record or draw are stored against your account. We do not currently derive approximate location from your IP address or network information for any purpose. You can control location permissions at any time through your device's operating system settings (iOS Settings > Privacy > Location Services; Android Settings > Apps > Permissions > Location). Disabling location access will prevent core safety features — including SOS, zone detection, hiker check-in, and live tracking — from functioning correctly. We do not recommend disabling location access while you are out in the field, as this may compromise your safety. **Sharing location with other people.** Certain features (such as hiker check-in, SOS, and party/"Where's My Mate" location sharing) are designed to share your location with emergency contacts or party members you nominate, or with Sentinel's systems for the purpose of alerting others if you do not check in as expected or if you or an overlapping hunter/hiker trigger an SOS. We do not make your live location publicly visible to other app users by default. **Community and research location data — not yet active.** Sentinel includes a "Contribute to Research" setting (see Section 4.3) that will, in future, share aggregated or de-identified sighting and activity data with conservation research bodies. As of the date of this Policy, this feature has not been activated: no data is currently being transmitted to any external research or government body, regardless of your setting. We will update this Policy before any such sharing begins. ### 2.3 Photos, Camera, and Media If you use Species ID, Field Journal, or photo attachment features, we access your camera and/or photo library to allow you to capture or upload images (for example, of game animals, sightings, or gear). Photos you submit for AI species identification may be processed by our AI service provider as described in Section 5. ### 2.4 Device, Usage, and Diagnostic Information - Device model, operating system and version, unique device identifiers, app version. - **Crash and error diagnostics**: if the App crashes or encounters an error, technical diagnostic information (such as stack traces and device state at the time of the error) is sent to our crash-reporting provider, Sentry, to help us identify and fix bugs. We configure Sentry to avoid capturing your IP address, GPS coordinates, and other personal content in these reports. See Section 6 for how long this data is retained. We do not currently operate a general usage-analytics system (such as tracking every screen you view or every feature you tap) beyond the crash/error diagnostics described above and the AI-credit usage records described in Section 5. ### 2.5 Health and Fitness Information If you use an Android device with Google Health Connect and choose to sync health data, Sentinel can read your **heart rate** and **step count** for the duration of a recorded hunt, hike, or DOC track completion, with your explicit permission granted through Health Connect. This is: - **Opt-in per recording**: you tap "Sync health data" for a specific completed track; nothing is read automatically or in the background. - **Stored against your account only**: heart rate and step data is saved to your own track/completion record, protected by the same access controls described in Section 7, and is never linked to or visible to any other user. - **Never shared externally**: this data is not sent to our AI features, not included in any research contribution, and not shared with any third party. - **Displayed only to you**, in your own History tab, alongside the relevant track. If you enable biometric login (fingerprint or face recognition) separately from Health Connect, authentication is handled entirely by your device's operating system (Apple Face ID/Touch ID, Android biometric APIs). Sentinel does not receive, store, or have access to your biometric data itself — only a confirmation that authentication succeeded or failed. ### 2.6 Advertising Free-tier use of Sentinel is partly supported by advertising, provided through Google AdMob. We request only **non-personalized** ads — Sentinel does not provide AdMob with information about you to target ads based on your behavior or interests. AdMob may still collect a device advertising identifier and basic technical information as needed to serve and measure non-personalized ads; see Section 12.2 and Google's own privacy policy for how Google handles this. ### 2.7 Information from Third Parties - **DOC (Department of Conservation) and Fish & Game NZ data**: publicly available track, zone, and hunting information we incorporate into the App is not personal information about you, but your interactions with it (e.g., favourites, visited tracks) are linked to your account. - **LINZ (Land Information New Zealand)**: property boundary and topographic data used for map overlays; this is not personal information about you. - **Weather and environmental data providers** (such as NIWA and OpenWeatherMap): used to provide forecasts and conditions; not linked to your identity. - **Google Maps**: map tiles and mapping services displayed in the App are provided by Google; see Section 4.1 and Section 12.2. ## 3. How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: - Provide core App functionality: mapping, zone detection, tracking, SOS alerts, hiker check-ins, and safety notifications. - Operate AI-powered features (Sentinel Companion, Hunt Report, Safety Assessment, Hunt Debrief, Gear Recommender, Trip Planner, and Species ID), as described in Section 5. - Maintain your account, process subscriptions and purchases, and manage the in-app credit system. - Award badges, track milestones, and manage gamified features. - Improve, troubleshoot, and secure the App, including crash reporting. - Display non-personalized advertising to free-tier users, as described in Section 2.6. - Communicate with you about your account, safety-related notices, and (where you have consented) product updates. - Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from authorities. We do not use your personal information to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you without human involvement. ## 4. How We Share Your Information We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances: ### 4.1 Service Providers We use third-party service providers to operate the App, including: - **Supabase** (database hosting and backend infrastructure) - **Anthropic** (AI model processing for AI-powered features — see Section 5) - **RevenueCat** (subscription and in-app purchase management) - **Apple App Store / Google Play** (app distribution and payment processing) - **Sentry** (crash reporting and error diagnostics) - **Google AdMob** (non-personalized advertising for free-tier users) - **Google Maps** (map tiles and mapping services) - **Resend** (transactional email, such as password resets) - **Expo / push notification services** (delivering notifications) These providers process data on our behalf and are contractually or contractually-equivalently restricted from using your information for their own purposes, except where a provider (such as Google, for AdMob or Maps) acts as an independent controller of limited technical data under its own privacy policy, as disclosed in Section 12.2. ### 4.2 Emergency Contacts and Safety Features If you use SOS or hiker check-in features, we share relevant location and status information with the emergency contacts you designate, and with other hunters or hikers whose active hunt zone or route genuinely overlaps with an SOS trigger location, so they can assist if nearby. We may also share information with emergency services if reasonably necessary to protect your life or safety or that of others. ### 4.3 Research and Government Partners — Not Yet Active Sentinel includes a "Contribute to Research" setting, first presented during onboarding and changeable at any time afterward in Profile settings. Once activated, this setting is intended to allow aggregated or de-identified sighting and activity data to be shared with research bodies such as the Department of Conservation (DOC) and Fish & Game New Zealand, to support wildlife management and conservation efforts. **This feature is not yet active.** We have not entered into a data-sharing arrangement with any research or government body, and no data is currently transmitted externally for this purpose regardless of your setting. Your choice is recorded so that, if and when a partnership is established, we can honour the preference you've already expressed — but nothing is shared today. We will update this Policy, and notify users in the App, before any such sharing begins. ### 4.4 Legal Requirements We may disclose information if required to do so by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Sentinel, our users, or the public. ### 4.5 Business Transfers If Sentinel is involved in a merger, acquisition, asset sale, or similar transaction, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change. ## 5. AI Features and Your Data Sentinel provides several AI-assisted features (Sentinel Companion, Hunt Report, Safety Assessment, Hunt Debrief, Gear Recommender, Trip Planner, and Species ID). To provide these features: - Relevant information you provide (such as chat messages, hunt details, location and weather context, or photos for species identification) is sent to our AI infrastructure provider, Anthropic, for processing. - We do not use your individual, identifiable conversations or submissions to train Anthropic's underlying AI models, and our agreements with Anthropic restrict the use of API data for that purpose. - **Sentinel Companion conversations are not stored** on our servers once a session ends; each conversation is processed and relayed without being saved. - **Generated Hunt Reports, Safety Assessments, and Gear Recommendations are cached** for efficiency and to avoid redundant processing, linked to the relevant zone, track, or query. These cached outputs are automatically deleted after 90 days — see Section 6. - AI features that involve photo submission (Species ID) only process images you actively choose to submit for that purpose. - AI outputs are generated by a machine-learning model and may occasionally be inaccurate or incomplete. AI-generated safety assessments, hunt debriefs, gear recommendations, and trip plans are provided for informational purposes only and should not be relied upon as a substitute for your own judgment, official safety advice, or emergency services. ## 6. Data Retention We retain personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App's services. Specifically: - **Account and profile data**: retained until you delete your account. - **Location tracks, zones, waypoints, and health data (heart rate/steps) attached to them**: retained until you delete them or delete your account, unless retention is required for safety, legal, or dispute-resolution purposes. - **Sentinel Companion conversations**: not stored server-side at all — see Section 5. - **Cached AI outputs** (Hunt Reports, Safety Assessments, Gear Recommendations): automatically deleted after 90 days. - **Crash and error diagnostics (Sentry)**: retained for approximately 30 days. - **Research data**: not currently collected — see Section 4.3. When you delete your account, we will delete or de-identify your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it by law or for legitimate business purposes such as fraud prevention or dispute resolution. ## 7. Data Security We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encryption in transit, database-level access controls, and row-level security on our backend (Supabase/PostgreSQL) — meaning your location tracks, health data, and other personal records are scoped so that only your own account can read or modify them. Biometric authentication, where enabled, never leaves your device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a privacy or security breach that has caused, or is likely to cause, serious harm, we will notify affected individuals and the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner in accordance with the Privacy Act 2020, and will notify other regulators or individuals as required by applicable law (including GDPR's 72-hour breach notification requirement where relevant). ## 8. International Data Transfers Sentinel is based in New Zealand, but some of our service providers (including Supabase, Anthropic, Sentry, Resend, and Google) may process or store data outside New Zealand, including in the United States or other jurisdictions. Where we transfer personal information internationally, we take reasonable steps to ensure it receives a comparable standard of protection, including through contractual safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where applicable to users in the EU/UK. ## 9. Children's Privacy Sentinel is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. Hunting and certain hiking activities may be subject to age restrictions under New Zealand law independent of this Policy. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without appropriate consent, we will take steps to delete that information. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details in Section 13. ## 10. Your Privacy Rights (New Zealand) Under the Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to: - **Access** the personal information we hold about you. - **Correct** personal information you believe is inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date. - Be informed about what personal information is collected and why, including when collected indirectly (in accordance with Information Privacy Principle 3A). To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13. We will respond as soon as reasonably practicable, and in any case within 20 working days, as required by law. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at [privacy.org.nz](https://www.privacy.org.nz). ## 11. Additional Rights for Users Outside New Zealand ### 11.1 European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland (GDPR/UK GDPR) If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, to data portability, to object to certain processing (including for direct marketing), and to withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent. Our legal bases for processing include performance of a contract (providing the App), consent (e.g., optional notifications), and legitimate interests (e.g., security, fraud prevention, service improvement). We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration, and we do not currently share personal information with third parties for research purposes (see Section 4.3). We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. ### 11.3 Other Jurisdictions If you reside in a jurisdiction with privacy laws not specifically addressed above (such as Australia's Privacy Act 1988 or Canada's PIPEDA), we aim to honour requests consistent with the rights described in this Policy to the extent reasonably practicable. Contact us using the details in Section 13. ## 12.
