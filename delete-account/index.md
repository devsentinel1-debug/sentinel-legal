# Delete Your Sentinel Account

Last updated: **31 July 2026**

This page explains how to request deletion of your Sentinel account and associated data.

## How to Delete Your Account

1. Open the **Sentinel** app and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile**.
3. Scroll to the **⚠️ Danger Zone** section at the bottom.
4. Tap **Delete my account**.
5. Type **DELETE** to confirm, then tap **Delete my account** again.

Your account and data are deleted immediately and permanently. You will be signed out automatically once deletion completes.

## Can't Access the App?

If you're unable to sign in or no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion by emailing us at <devsentinel1@gmail.com> from the email address associated with your account. Please include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line. We will process your request and confirm once it's complete, typically within a few business days.

## What Gets Deleted

Requesting account deletion permanently removes:

- Your account and login credentials
- All hunt zones and GPS tracks
- All logged kills and sightings
- All photos in your Field Journal
- Your AI credit balance and transaction history
- Waypoints, trip plans, and saved gear locker items
- Party and location-sharing connections with other users

## Retention Period

Deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no retention period, and data is not recoverable once deleted. The only exception is where we are required to retain limited information by law or for legitimate purposes such as fraud prevention or dispute resolution, consistent with Section 6 of our [Privacy Policy](https://devsentinel1-debug.github.io/sentinel-legal/privacy/).
